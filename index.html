Flagship Product · Co-founder

XparkAI

A hyperlocal healthcare logistics platform for Tier-2 cities — connecting patients with nearby pharmacies, blood units and hospital beds in real time, starting in Bhubaneswar.

THE PRODUCT

What I owned

  • Product strategy. Co-led the strategy for a scalable Tier-2 healthcare ecosystem — specifying and prioritising features across a patient app and a pharmacy-partner app.
  • Core mechanic. A prescription is broadcast to pharmacies within a 3 km geofence for 10 minutes, and nearby partners bid to fulfil it — turning a phone-around search into a single request.
  • Trust by design. Chose a hybrid architecture that keeps user data separate from location data, with compliance guardrails around the AI assistant to manage risk.
  • Business case. Built the pitch ecosystem — deck, Business Model Canvas, financials and pitch script.
UNDER THE HOOD

How it's built

A dual-app Flutter platform on a hybrid Firebase Firestore + Supabase PostgreSQL backend, with Haversine-based proximity matching and a three-layer AI assistant powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash. Built as a six-member team at KIIT under the guidance of Dr. Mainak Biswas, with an accompanying research paper for IEEE Access.

★ PRODUCT MANAGEMENT LIVE PROJECT · PHOENIX GLOBAL · JUN–JUL 2026

STC Mobile — The Sovereign Digital Ecosystem

A product-market-fit strategy for an STC-branded smartphone in Saudi Arabia.

Rather than competing on hardware specs, I positioned the device as Saudi Arabia's digital-identity phone — integrated by default with mystc and stc pay so communications, payments and entertainment live in one place. The framework covers product purpose, customer needs, feature prioritisation, competitive positioning and performance metrics.

  • Customer needs: functional and emotional jobs-to-be-done, mapped to everyday usage situations — payments, streaming, gaming, work, family plans.
  • Prioritisation: must / should / could-have features, led by deep ecosystem integration, a 5G optimisation profile and native BNPL financing.
  • The moat: structural integration with STC's network and financial rails, which generic handsets can't replicate.
